# Source Selection And Conflict Handling

Use the source type that is authoritative for the claim:

| Topic | Primary evidence | Supporting evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulation, compliance, public programs | Statutes, regulations, agencies, courts, enforcement actions | Legal analysis, practitioner experience, news |
| Market or company size | Filings, annual reports, investor materials, methodology-backed data | Trade reporting, reputable analysis |
| Product behavior or pricing | Official documentation, changelogs, terms, live product pages | Reviews, support forums, user reports |
| Technical implementation | Official documentation, source code, maintainer discussions | Issues, Stack Overflow, production case studies |
| Research evidence | Systematic reviews, peer-reviewed work, original datasets | Preprints, expert interpretation |

Deprioritize undated pages, content farms, affiliate roundups, unattributed
statistics, and sources with an unacknowledged commercial incentive. For each
key claim, prefer three independent source types for high confidence, two for
medium confidence, and label a single-source claim low confidence. When sources
conflict, state the conflict, compare scope, date, methodology, and incentives,
then preserve uncertainty if it cannot be resolved.
