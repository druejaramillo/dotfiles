# Research Playbooks

## Market Entry

Triangulate market size with top-down, bottom-up, and analogous-market methods.
Map direct, indirect, and substitute competitors. Test customer demand, business
models, entry requirements, timing, failure cases, and unit-economics
assumptions. Deliver go, no-go, or conditional-entry criteria rather than a
generic market overview.

## Product Or Feature

Define the root problem before comparing solutions. Map build, buy, and
integrate options. Evaluate capability, total cost, integration burden,
maintenance, adoption, negative user feedback, and implementation failures.
Deliver a weighted decision matrix with assumptions exposed.

## Competitive Analysis

Build the competitive set across direct, indirect, substitute, and prospective
entrants. Analyze positioning, pricing, customer segment, product velocity,
reviews, hiring, partnerships, and financial signals. Separate a competitor's
claims from evidence of customer experience. Look for underserved segments and
likely next moves.

## Regulatory Or Compliance

Start with the controlling law, regulation, agency guidance, and effective
date. Then examine state or local variation, enforcement actions, court
decisions, practitioner interpretation, and pending changes. Distinguish the
written rule from enforcement reality. Deliver a requirement, risk, and
mitigation matrix; research is not legal advice.

## Problem Solving

Use root-cause analysis to avoid researching the symptom. Quantify the impact,
map conventional and analogous solutions, and find comparable case studies,
including failures. Compare effectiveness, cost, time, risk, prerequisites, and
transferability. Separate quick wins from strategic bets.

## Service Expansion

Screen candidate services by demand, regulatory feasibility, operational fit,
revenue potential, and competition. Deep-dive survivors for cross-sell fit,
delivery requirements, margin, brand risk, and dependencies. Deliver a
prioritized sequence, not an unranked option list.
