# Framework Selection

Select one to three frameworks because they reveal a decision-relevant
structure. Do not add a framework as a decorative checklist.

| Research question | Primary frameworks | Useful output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market entry or new vertical | PESTEL, Five Forces, market segmentation | Entry conditions and timing |
| Product, feature, or tool choice | Jobs to Be Done, build-buy-integrate, value proposition canvas | Decision matrix and tradeoffs |
| Competitive landscape | Positioning map, Five Forces, SWOT | Whitespace and defensible advantage |
| Regulation or policy | Regulatory landscape map, PESTEL | Requirement and enforcement-risk matrix |
| Operational problem | Root-cause analysis, analogous-industry scan | Tested intervention options |
| Service expansion | Value proposition canvas, segmentation, business model canvas | Priority and sequencing matrix |

For every option in a framework, answer: when does it win, what does it
sacrifice, who chooses it, and what evidence supports that conclusion. A
framework is incomplete if it does not change a decision or expose a tradeoff.
