# Exhaust the design space

Read when a consequential novel interaction or engineering choice has multiple
viable shapes and no established precedent. Compare concrete alternatives before
committing to the expensive implementation.

Build or sketch two or three genuinely different shapes and compare them side by
side. A cosmetic variation of the first design does not count. Use the existing
prototype/design workflow; independent workers are optional and require authority,
isolated outputs, and budget. Exploration remains within the requested scope.

## Applies to

- Novel UI interactions with no codebase precedent.
- Architectural choices with multiple viable approaches.
- Product design whose experience depends on feel as well as logic.

## Keep the direct path for

- Mechanical implementation with an established pattern.
- A bug fix or refactor with a clear target state.
- Constraints that leave only one viable approach.

Prototype observations inform the decision; they do not authorize a change in
product direction or an approved concept contract. Preserve the existing shaping
and design gates. The point is to avoid building the wrong thing, not to force a
design contest at every function boundary.
