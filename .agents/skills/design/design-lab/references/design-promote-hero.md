# Promote hero

Require a full-page prototype **and** a chosen hero study. Inspect both,
approved content, and the existing lab. Integrate the selected hero as the
page's first viewport; preserve its settled composition and the lab, picker,
alternative heroes, and comparison routes. Build downstream content only from
approved product facts and within the page's established visual grammar.

Motion may enhance visible content but must not hide the bridge or downstream
sections when JavaScript or reduced motion is in effect. Do not gate body
content behind intersection observers, opacity, clipping, or delayed reveal
state. Protect transformed display typography from clipping. Keep the
hero-to-body transition continuous and visibly populated. Retest interest-form
invalid, success, and reset states if the prototype has one.

Use `web_screenshot` on the actual served route at desktop and mobile sizes
**after entrance motion settles**; inspect glyphs, image crops, and overflow.
Browser-check the hero/body boundary, one downstream section, and any form
states. Check development and production preview routes, source-truth
compliance, detector findings where available, syntax, formatting, and project
build. Report preserved exploration paths alongside the promoted page; stop
before production-home promotion.
