# Review research

Review the actual paper, proposal, report or draft before its reputation or a
summary of it. Inspect linked evidence when central claims depend on it.

Check argument clarity, evidence quality, alternatives, quantitative support,
traceability, stale sources, tables/figures and unsupported certainty. Empirical
work also needs attention to baselines, ablations, leakage, sample size and
implementation detail. Decision reports need assumptions, incentives, costs,
real-world implementation and affected stakeholders.

Separate a flaw from an unavailable check. Cite exact passages or sections;
mark unseen sections blocked rather than imagining their contents. Use
FATAL, MAJOR and MINOR only where the consequences justify those labels.

Use the requested location or `outputs/<slug>-review.md` for substantive work.
Give a short assessment, evidence-backed strengths, prioritized findings,
questions, revision order and inspected/blocked sources. Do not predict venue
acceptance. Optional independent Herdr review follows the common rules; do not
call review of your own draft independent.
