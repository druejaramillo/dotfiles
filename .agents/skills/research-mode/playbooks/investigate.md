# Investigate

Resolve an open question from evidence, at the smallest useful depth.

1. Pin the question, intended use, relevant date/geography and exclusions from
   the request. Decompose a broad question into decision-relevant subquestions;
   a narrow question can go straight to sources.
2. Search genuinely different angles and source families. Follow primary
   sources for pivotal claims and the strongest opposing case. Use the
   scholarly/PDF references when their conditions apply.
3. Explain what the evidence establishes, what conflicts and what remains
   unknown. Compare alternative explanations for material conclusions.
4. Check important quotations, numbers and source support in the finished
   answer. Name blocked checks and do not claim full coverage from snippets.

Answer in chat for a small question. For substantive work, use the user's
location or `outputs/<slug>.md` with the answer, scope/method, findings,
tradeoffs, opposing evidence, uncertainties, sources and verification limits.
Optional planning/working notes must not become a pre-search approval gate.
