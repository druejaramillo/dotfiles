# Evidence-grounded drafting

Establish the audience, requested form and supplied research files. Use the
existing evidence first. Gather missing background where appropriate or label
the gap; do not promote tentative notes into established findings.

Write an academic paper to the requested location or `papers/<slug>.md`;
other research drafts default to `outputs/<slug>-draft.md`. Preserve a
project's established location and honor chat-only requests.

Adapt structure to the document's job. A paper may need abstract, problem,
related work, method, evidence/results, limitations and conclusion; a decision
report needs an answer, evidence, options, consequences and limits. Every
quantitative table, graph or image must trace to a source, raw artifact or
reproducible calculation. Missing experiments remain proposed work.

For technical explanations, consult upstream
`~/.agents/skills/pstack/technical-writing/SKILL.md` as needed for clarity.
Keep the requested form, citations, scientific uncertainty and limitations
authoritative rather than forcing a paper into a documentation template.

Check pivotal claims/citations in the finished draft. Optional independent
review examines a fixed draft; the calling agent owns the final document.
Outline approval is needed only when the user requested that stop.
