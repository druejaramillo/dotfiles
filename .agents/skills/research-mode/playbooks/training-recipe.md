# Practical training or technical recipe

Find implementable, evidence-backed approaches for the requested result.
Do not assume a non-ML question needs an ML solution.

1. Establish constraints and the success metric. Connect each candidate to an
   observed result, or label it an untested proposal.
2. Inspect required inputs/data, implementation paths, versions, parameters,
   cost/compute, limitations and verification status.
3. For ML, inspect dataset/checkpoint access and licenses, splits/schema,
   benchmark fit, hyperparameters, training method and evaluation. A cited
   script does not establish a score; a reported score does not establish
   that the data remains accessible.
4. Rank by applicability, feasibility and measured quality. Outline the
   smallest implementation and its unresolved prerequisites.

Use the requested location or `outputs/<slug>-recipe.md`. Include a
recommendation, ranked evidence table, implementation outline, prerequisites
and verified/unverified/blocked/inferred status. Finding a recipe does not
authorize executing it; execution uses the separate execution rules.
