# PDF reading

1. Identify document/version and confirm access. Use available PDF tools or
   check for pdftotext before choosing it. Read bounded page ranges for long
   files; a truncated extraction is not complete coverage.
2. Map abstract, methods, results, tables, figures, captions and supplements.
   Follow cross-references before accepting a metric or causal claim.
3. Cite quantitative or disputed claims by page/table/figure or exact passage.
   Distinguish PDF page indexes from printed page numbers when they differ.
   Check units, sample sizes, methods and whether graphics are illustrative.
4. If extraction loses columns, equations or labels, inspect a rendered page
   with an available viewer/image tool. Otherwise label the visual check
   blocked. OCR/access may be unavailable; never reconstruct unseen text.
5. Keep brief page-located notes when they help a large investigation. A
   source summary reports the source's claims, not independent validation.
