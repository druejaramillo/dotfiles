# Literature Review

Read `../deep-research/references/evidence.md` for claim and citation standards. Read `../alpha-research/SKILL.md` when scholarly search is needed; use `../pdf-explore/SKILL.md` for methods/figures that depend on PDF pages.

For a topic, identify scope, relevant period, canonical and recent papers, methodological families, independent replications, disagreement, and open questions. Search across terms, not just one ranking. Prefer paper pages with stable DOI/arXiv/PMID identifiers and read full methods/results for papers that carry the conclusion; abstracts alone do not establish details.

For a lab or author, resolve identity before attributing papers. Gather a bounded reachable publication list, indicate coverage and missing records, map 3–5 meaningful trajectories if warranted, and explain why selected papers changed direction relative to prior work; do not rank by prestige alone.

Write `outputs/<slug>-lit.md` with scope/method, a source-linked map of key work (year and identifier), consensus and disagreement, method/evidence limitations, next reading or testable follow-ups, and coverage/verification gaps. An optional plan or publication notes file is fine for a large corpus; do not require a plan approval gate. Cite paper claims directly, distinguish a preprint from a peer-reviewed version, and do a distinct citation check before finishing. Use the default Pi agent; an independent Herdr review is optional for contentious findings.
