# Kick off this Shape Up pitch

${@:-Use the approved pitch from this conversation.}

Read the full pitch and any linked concept specs. If the pitch or build
decision is missing, ask for it; kickoff is not permission to place the bet.
Read `~/.pi/agent/skills/feature-shaping/references/shaping-tools.md` for the
DUMP and AFFINITIZE kickoff exercise. If the pitch names concepts or
synchronizations, read `~/.pi/agent/references/concept-integrity.md` as well.

Walk through the problem, appetite, solution, rabbit holes, and no-gos.
Surface questions for the shaper and builders rather than guessing answers.
Help builders dump imagined tasks and cluster them into a few **initially
unnamed** groups, then name tentative integrated scopes. Identify which scopes
remain uphill and propose an early, risky end-to-end slice that could
demonstrate real behavior. The map is provisional: the team owns implementation
and will discover or redraw scopes and tasks while building. Do not preassign
tasks, publish tickets, or begin coding just because kickoff was requested.
